Work With Me

Commissions

I accept a small number of commissions each year. Each piece is made with care and full attention.

Step 1

Vision Details

Share the story, mood, or palette you'd love to see through the contact form. The more details you provide, the better I can shape the artwork to reflect your world.

Step 2

Creation & Payment

Your description is transformed into a one-of-a-kind painting, crafted with care and imagination. Completion usually takes 4-6 weeks. Payment in euros is required in advance via PayPal, though alternatives such as bank transfer or Wise are available to avoid fees.

Step 3

Completion and delivery

Once finished, the artwork is varnished, prepared and shipped worldwide. Each piece includes a Certificate of Authenticity.
